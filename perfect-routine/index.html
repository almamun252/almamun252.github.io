▤

▧

Notes & categories

Attach images to task notes, create categories, and organize task information your way.

Simple by design

Your work stays yours.

Perfect Routine stores tasks, categories, focus-session records, and attached images in local storage. This project's Android manifest does not declare the INTERNET permission.

⌘

Designed for Android

Native Android experience built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose.

  • Minimum Android version: 8.0 (API 26)
  • Local database powered by Room
  • Notifications and focus-session service
ⓘ

Before installing

Reminder delivery may depend on your Android version, notification permissions, exact-alarm access, and device battery settings.

Download the APK only from the official GitHub release. Review the release notes and app permissions before installing.
